Models: blade element momentum theory for spin (M1), Gutin's tonal formula with A-weighting for the hum (M2), and a sum over blade positions for the notes (M3), and Zwicker loudness, DIN sharpness, roughness and psychoacoustic annoyance from MoSQITo for the scores (M4, computed in Python; the whoosh is a labelled guess). Blade widths are estimates until the real blades are measured. Only the hum is modelled; the whoosh (broadband noise) is not.

Compared with Meister, Van Renterghem & Pieren, Acta Acustica 2026. Code: github.com/kivoon-dev/Propeller-Noice-Sim
