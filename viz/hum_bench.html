What this model leaves out

  • Only the steady hum (tones). The hiss from turbulent air over the blades is missing. It sits above 2-4 kHz, where Empa found the toroidal propeller differs.
  • All blade force is squeezed into one point at 80% of the radius. That is why the intake side can cancel almost perfectly, which real blades do not.
  • Blade widths are estimates until the real blades are measured, so RPM may be off by 10-20%. The ordering between the two props is the trustworthy part.
  • The tones you hear are pure sine waves at the predicted levels. Real propellers also buzz from motor noise and blade-to-blade differences.

Where the numbers come from

This page runs the same model as the Python code in propeller-noise-sim: thrust and power coefficients from the Milestone 1 blade-element model (bemt.py), then Gutin's formula with A-weighting from Milestone 2 (gutin.py).
